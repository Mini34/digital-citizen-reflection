Make the activity work for you

Use the keyboard, adjust the reading options, or take the worksheet to paper.

Choose how you read

Open Accessibility options near the top of any page for larger text, more text spacing, stronger contrast, or reduced motion. Light and Dark modes are available from Appearance in the header. Your device's reduced-motion setting is always respected. You can also use your browser's zoom. Reset reading options restores the standard reading choices without clearing your reflection.

Use the keyboard

Press Tab to move forward and Shift + Tab to move back. The first link skips to the main content. Press Enter on a button or link. Press Space on a checkbox or a disclosure such as Accessibility options. Use your arrow keys to choose a topic, appearance mode, or text size. A visible outline shows where you are.

Reflection step buttons let you move backward and edit your answers. Help opens only when you request it. Press Escape inside help or Accessibility options to close it and return to its opening button. You can also use Close help.

Read with assistive technology

Fields have visible labels and associated hints. The current step is identified in text and announced when you navigate. Missing answers have a written explanation linked to the field. Source links name the source they open. The time comparison is available as text; its bars repeat the same information. The QR code has a text description and an equivalent activity link.

Prefer paper or plain text?

Download the one-page worksheet (PDF), or print the activity page with JavaScript disabled. After completing an action plan, you can download its plain text or print it. Presentation scripts are available as web text alongside the PowerPoint and PDFs.

Your pace and your words

There is no deadline for an answer. Short responses, uncertainty, and unchanged habits are welcome. Optional help offers do not open automatically or move your focus. No thanks stops proactive offers for the current visit. Viewing options do not change your answers or enable draft saving.
